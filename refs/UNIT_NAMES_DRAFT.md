# Unit names — DRAFT, UNAPPROVED (§0.7 / §20)

**Nothing here renders until you approve it.** 60 names, 12 courses × 5 units of 5 lessons.

## Why these exist

Screen audit, `refs/audit/20260810/FINDINGS.md` → LEARN, finding 1, **4/4 convergence**: every
comparator scopes the visible lesson list to a UNIT with its own name, and carries two levels — the
course, then a named group of ~5–12 lessons you are inside right now. Euphoria carries one, and
shows all 25 lessons in a flat scroll with nine consecutive padlocks.

```
Duolingo   SECTION 1, UNIT 1  ·  "Learn Korean vowels"
Speak      UNIT 1 · 12 LESSONS ·  "Starting a Conversation"
Babbel     Newcomer I (A1.1) - Unit 1 · "Greet people and say goodbye"
Learna     "Beginner" as a section rule
```

## The structure is already there

Fives are not arbitrary. **Lesson 10 is a Capstone or Foundations Checkpoint in all 12 courses, and
lesson 25 is an Advanced Capstone in all 12.** Five units of five puts each capstone exactly at a
unit boundary — U2 closes on the checkpoint, U5 closes on the advanced capstone. No content moves.

## How these were written

Each name is derived from the five authored lesson titles it covers, nothing else. Aimed at the
comparator register — short, plain, concrete: Duolingo's "Learn Korean vowels", Babbel's "Greet
people and say goodbye". 2–5 words, no jargon the unit itself teaches.

**They are my drafts, not authored copy.** §0.7 forbids inventing product copy, so this file is a
proposal. Approve, edit, or reject any line.

## Ones I am least confident in, flagged rather than hidden

- **Quantitative Analysis U5, "The Deep End"** — covers risk-neutral pricing, copulas, Bayesian
  methods and quantum computing. Honest about difficulty, but it is the only name that describes how
  hard the unit is rather than what it is about. Alternative: "Measure, Dependence and What's Next".
- **Personal Finance U2, "Debt, Tax and Cover"** — "cover" for insurance is more British than the
  rest of the app's register. Alternative: "Debt, Tax and Insurance".
- **Behavioral Finance U5, "Building Your Defences"** — the unit is part defence (debiasing) and part
  theory (behavioral portfolio theory, market anomalies), so the name leans on one half.
- **Investing Fundamentals U5, "Practise With Fake Money"** — the plainest name here and the closest
  to how a 19-year-old would say it, which may read as too casual beside the other eleven courses.

## Where these would render

`learn.tsx`, as a section header above each group of five, plus a divider between units. No change to
lesson data — a static 12×5 map keyed by course id, since `PathwayLesson` has no unit field and
`courses/*.ts` is auto-synced and must not be edited.
